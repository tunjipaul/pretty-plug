How do I schedule a home service in Victoria Island?

expand_more
Home services can be booked directly through our portal. Simply select the "Mobile Service" option during checkout. Please note that mobile services are currently exclusive to Victoria Island, Ikoyi, and Lekki Phase 1, and incur a standard concierge fee of ₦15,000.

Can I book for multiple people at once?

expand_more
Yes! For group bookings of 3 or more (Bridal parties, Birthdays), please contact our concierge team via WhatsApp directly. We offer exclusive packages for groups that include complimentary refreshments.
